Case 1 (PASS) - Avionics Wire Ampacity & Resistance
Transcript: "Routed a new AWG 16 conductor utilizing a 19/29 stranding configuration. The direct current resistance of the wire was measured at 3.95 ohms per 1000 feet at 20C. Peak current load under test is 11.5 amperes in free air."


Case 2 (FAIL) - Avionics Wire Ampacity Exceeded
Transcript: "Completed installation of an AWG 20 conductor with 19/32 stranding. Measured wire resistance is 9.80 ohms per 1000 feet at 20C. The circuit drew a continuous current load of 8.0 amperes in free air."


Case 3 (PASS) - Ground Stud Torque & Bond Resistance
Transcript: "Installed a 10-32 steel ground stud nut on a Class R electrical return path. Torqued the grounding hardware to 22.5 inch-pounds. The electrical bonding resistance of the stud assembly measured 1.9 milliohms."


Case 4 (FAIL) - Under-torqued 1/4-28 Ground Stud Nut
Transcript: "Completed grounding termination on a Class C lightning protection structure. Installed a 1/4-28 ground stud nut and torqued the assembly to 32.0 inch-pounds. Measured bonding resistance is 1.1 milliohms."


Case 5 (PASS) - 1/2-inch CRES Flareless Tubing Torque & Clearance
Transcript: "Completed installation of a 1/2-inch CRES flareless tube line. Torqued the B-nut fitting to 200 inch-pounds using a dial torque wrench. Measured lateral clearance to the adjacent wire bundle is 0.75 inches, and separation from the flight control cables is 2.5 inches."


Case 6 (FAIL) - Tubing Clearance to Wire Harness Too Small
Transcript: "Installed a 1/2-inch CRES flareless line on the return side. Torqued the fitting B-nut to 190 inch-pounds. Measured separation from the moving flight control cables is 2.2 inches. Clearance from the adjacent electrical wire harness is 0.35 inches."


Case 7 (PASS) - Cleveland Wheel Tie-bolts Star Pattern Torque
Transcript: "Assembled split wheel halves of Cleveland wheel hub. Torqued the threaded tie-bolts in a multi-stage sequential star pattern to a final specification of 350 inch-pounds using a dial torque wrench. Secured the assembly with lockwire."


Case 8 (FAIL) - Over-torqued Cleveland Wheel Tie-bolts
Transcript: "Completed assembly of Cleveland split wheel hub sections. Torqued all high-strength tie-bolts in a star pattern to a final torque of 650 inch-pounds using a dial torque wrench. Safety wire has been applied."


Case 9 (PASS) - Spark Plug Mating Thread Torque & Gap
Transcript: "Installed 18mm Champion spark plugs in the engine cylinder. Verified the electrode gaps using a wire thickness gauge at 0.018 inches. Torqued the spark plug threads to 27 foot-pounds and applied approved anti-seize compound."
